The order of operations

People. Process. Technology.
In that order, deliberately.

Technology should support a system that already has clear ownership and defined rules—not compensate for their absence.

01

People

Assign one accountable owner to each stage and establish an internal sponsor for adoption.

Output: accountability
02

Process

Define qualification, terminology, routing, follow-up, and the conditions for every handoff.

Output: repeatability
03

Technology

Configure the CRM, integrations, automation, and reporting around the way the firm should work.

Output: visibility

Technology with judgment

HubSpot is powerful.
That doesn’t make it automatic.

Its flexibility can support a highly tailored legal-intake system. It can also create bloated pipelines, fragile workflows, and reporting nobody trusts.

The right CRM is the one the firm can adopt and operate consistently. Sometimes the better technical decision is the simpler one.

Your tech stack isn’t a collection. Every tool needs a job.
